What to look for

  • In calm air the leaves are sharp, separate splats. In wind the static model averages the moving leaves into a larger, blurrier canopy.
  • The images still look plausible (PSNR drops only a few %), but the geometry does not: F-score falls sharply and the per-leaf centroid error grows.
  • Sensor noise mainly gives fewer, larger Gaussians: coverage (recall) goes up, precision does not.
