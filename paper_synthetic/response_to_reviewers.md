# Response to reviewers

**Manuscript:** How much does wind cost? A paired calm/windy synthetic RGB-D benchmark that separates occlusion, sensor and wind error in simulated 3D foliage reconstruction

We thank the reviewer for a careful and constructive report. Below, each point is quoted in short form and followed by our response and the change made. Every number in the manuscript is generated by `scripts/paper/make_assets.py` from the per-run result files in the repository, so the revised statistics can be checked directly.

---

## Major comments

### 1. External validity: claims should be restricted to simulation
We agree. All conclusions are now explicitly scoped to simulation under the default sensor and wind models and to the methods tested:
- **Title:** "…in *simulated* 3D foliage reconstruction".
- **Abstract:** the closing sentence now says the findings hold in simulation under these models, without motion blur or rolling shutter, and remain to be confirmed on real captures.
- **Contribution 4 and Conclusion:** now begin "In simulation, under default sensor and wind models and for the methods tested…".
- **Discussion:** adds that absolute magnitudes may differ on real data and that the wind errors are likely lower bounds for real RGB capture (see minor comment on motion blur).

### 2. Accounting of runs and sequences
- **Wind levels:** the protocol now states that each tree is rendered at four canopy wind speeds, U ∈ {0, 1.5, 3, 5} m/s, with U = 0 being the calm sequence. That gives 4 × 10 = 40 sequences. The confusing "calm and at 0, 1.5, 3, 5" wording is gone. The tables also define low/medium/high as 1.5/3/5 m/s.
- **Runs:** a new **Table (Accounting of all training runs)** lists each study, method, trees, the wind speeds used per sensor variant, seeds and runs. The total is **108 runs**: 60 benchmark runs (48 splatfacto, 12 depth-nerfacto) and 48 baseline-study runs (16 each for static, trimmed-loss and deformable 3DGS). The six benchmark conditions are now listed explicitly: noisy sensor at all four speeds, and clean sensor at calm and 5 m/s.
- A superseded first round of 16 deformable runs (six-band temporal encoding) is mentioned in the text and table caption but not counted.

### 3. Held-out generalisation claimed but not reported
We agree that this was misleading. The text now states that all experiments use trees 1, 3, 5 and 7, one per leaf type. Trees 8 and 9 are designated as a test split for *future* generalisation studies and are not used in this paper, and the text says that no generalisation experiments are reported. The caption of the dataset table was changed accordingly.

### 4. Thin statistics and "approximately additive"
- **Interaction:** we removed "approximately additive". The text now reports the splatfacto interaction at 5 m/s in both chamfer distance (+1.4 ± 1.5 mm) and centroid error (−0.8 ± 2.1 mm) next to the corresponding wind terms. For depth-nerfacto (two trees) it is +2.2 ± 3.1 mm. We state that the spread is as large as the mean, so four trees show no evidence of strong non-additivity, which is weaker than establishing additivity.
- **Monotonicity:** this was previously claimed "on all four trees". The text now says the F-score decreases monotonically on seed means for all four trees, and per seed in 7 of 8 tree–seed pairs.
- **Trimmed loss:** it wins in 16/16 conditions. We added a one-sided sign test, p = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵, with the caveat that conditions share four trees and are therefore not fully independent.
- **Rest-pose vs posed:** posed F exceeds rest-pose F in 10 of 12 windy conditions; this count is now reported.
- **Error bars:** captions now explain that seeds are averaged first and error bars are ± one std over trees. They therefore reflect tree-to-tree variability (leaf type, density, motion), not seed noise; the between-seed std of F is ≈ 0.014.

### 5. Baseline scope
The Discussion now states that our conclusions concern the tested implementations only: Nerfstudio splatfacto and depth-nerfacto, and our gsplat-based static, trimmed-loss and deformable 3DGS. It also states that 2DGS, other NeRF variants, RGB-D SLAM and dedicated dynamic Gaussian methods were not evaluated. The abstract and conclusion now refer to "the deformation-field baseline we tested". We agree that a 2DGS and a dedicated dynamic baseline would strengthen the paper. The released pipeline and per-leaf ground truth are designed to make such evaluations straightforward, and we list them as future work.

### 6. Reproducibility
- **Code** is public at https://github.com/bibekgyawali2/synthtree, and an MIT `LICENSE` file has been added to the repository.
- **Results:** per-run results for all 108 runs are in the repository as CSV files. A single script (`scripts/paper/make_assets.py`) regenerates every table, figure and in-text number. The Kaggle training scripts are under `kaggle/`.
- **Dataset** (40 sequences, CC BY 4.0) is available to editors and reviewers on request and will be made public with a DOI on publication. It can also be regenerated bit-for-bit (geometry, poses and ground truth) from the released configuration files.
- **Affiliation and contact details** will be completed in the final submission. *(Author note: still to be filled in.)*

### 7. Table 4: dashes for robust3dgs and sign convention
- **Dashes:** the robust3dgs rows with dashes came from a bug in table generation: baseline-study runs leaked into the benchmark table. They have been removed. Trimmed-loss results appear only in the baseline-study table, where they belong.
- **Sign convention:** column headers now read "F@5mm drop", "CD increase" and "Centroid increase". The caption defines that every component except the absolute occlusion/method row is a signed error increase. Positive means worse, so a positive F entry is a decrease of the F-score and a negative entry (e.g. the splatfacto sensor term) is an apparent improvement.

## Minor comments

- **"sensor-matched" → "D435i-like":** changed throughout. The text also says the intrinsics are nominal values that can be replaced by a calibrated device's values.
- **Duplicate calm / 0 m/s:** clarified that U = 0 *is* the calm sequence (see 2).
- **Motion blur / rolling shutter:** now stated prominently in three places: the rendering section (in emphasis), the protocol ("disabled throughout") and the Discussion, which notes that the reported wind errors are likely lower bounds. The abstract also mentions it.
- **Precision/recall in Table 3:** Prec. and Rec. columns added. The caption defines all columns and the averaging.
- **Motion vs degradation per tree:** the Results now give, per tree, the median leaf displacement at 5 m/s with the absolute and relative F drop. The Spearman correlation is ρ = 1.00 for the absolute drop and ρ = 0.40 for the relative drop. With n = 4 trees this is descriptive only, and leaf type and density covary with motion.
- **Rest-pose result is method-specific:** the abstract, results and conclusion now attribute the 34 % over-statement to our static 3DGS baseline (four trees, one seed, noisy input) and state that other static methods were not tested.
- **Abstract centroid claim:** now specifies "the per-leaf centroid error of splatfacto … at 5 m/s".
- **Related work:** extended in two places.
  - Plant motion and spatio-temporal phenotyping: growth tracking from 4D point clouds (Li et al., 2013) and wind biomechanics (de Langre, 2008).
  - Robust neural reconstruction: NeRF in the Wild (Martin-Brualla et al., 2021) and RobustNeRF (Sabour et al., 2023), which motivates our trimmed loss.
  - DOIs for all new references were verified via Crossref.
- **Figure captions:** all result figures now explain the error bars and tree-level variability (see 4).

A few remarks appear to stem from text-extraction artefacts in the reviewed PDF (garbled symbols or merged words) rather than from the manuscript itself; we have not changed the text in response to those.
