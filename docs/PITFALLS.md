# Known pitfalls and how this pipeline handles them

| # | Pitfall | What goes wrong | How it is handled here | Verified by |
|---|---------|-----------------|------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Axis conventions** (Blender/OpenGL camera looks −Z with +Y up; OpenCV looks +Z with +Y down) | cameras look backwards, mirrored reconstructions, PSNR ~10 dB | One conversion `c2w_cv = c2w_blender @ diag(1,−1,−1,1)` in `synthtree/transforms.py`; transforms.json = OpenGL c2w, TUM / `c2w_opencv.npy` = OpenCV c2w; world is always Blender's (+Z up) | `tests/test_conventions.py`; per-frame self-reprojection of the world-position pass (< 0.002 px); cross-frame depth reprojection |
| 2 | **Principal point / sensor fit / pixel aspect** | sub-pixel to multi-pixel offsets that look like pose noise | `intrinsics.to_blender` derives lens/shift/pixel-aspect from Blender's `compute_viewplane` with `sensor_fit=HORIZONTAL` and the OpenCV pixel-centre convention (centre = (W−1)/2) | round-trip unit test + world positions project onto their own pixel centres to 1e-3 px in real renders |
| 3 | **z-depth vs ray distance** | depth "bends" at image corners (factor up to 1.16 at 70° FOV) | Depth is computed by us as z = (P − C)·optical axis from an exact world-position AOV, so Blender's own definition is irrelevant. (Cycles' Depth pass in 4.5 and 5.2 *is* planar — reported in qc.json) | `ground_plane` check: back-projected floor stays on z = 0 within µm in the corners; ray distance would be ~18 cm off |
| 4 | **Anti-aliasing mixes depth / ids at edges** | flying pixels and fractional leaf ids in the *ground truth* | GT passes come from a separate Cycles render with 1 sample and a 0.01 px box filter: every value is the first hit of the ray through the pixel centre | leaf ids are exact integers; `leaf_id>0 ⇔ semantic==leaf` with 0 mismatches |
| 5 | **Half-float EXR** | ids > 2048 and positions lose precision | data EXRs are written with `color_depth='32'` | id integrality check in qc |
| 6 | **Blender 5.x writes multi-part EXR** | readers that only open part 0 see just `Combined` (all passes "missing") | `synthtree.imageio.read_exr` iterates over all parts (also handles 4.5's single-part layout) | smoke run on 4.5 and 5.2 |
| 7 | **Colour management** (Filmic/AgX tone mapping, dither noise) | RGB not comparable to a sensor; non-deterministic 8-bit noise | View transform `Standard`, look None, exposure 0, gamma 1, dither 0; beauty saved as linear EXR and converted with the exact sRGB OETF | `blender/check_render_setup.py` + `scripts/check_colour.py`: ≤ 1 LSB vs Blender's own PNG on 4.5 and 5.2 |
| 8 | **Instancing / attribute loss** | per-leaf ids and attachment points vanish when instances are realised | no instancing and no Geometry Nodes: leaves are one real mesh with FACE attributes (`leaf_id`, `leaf_id_f`, colours) and POINT attributes (`P_rest`, `P_next`) written directly | face count asserted on import; id check |
| 9 | **EEVEE + alpha-blended leaves** | depth/normal passes ignore alpha-blended cut-outs; silhouettes wrong | leaves are real geometry (no alpha anywhere); GT comes from Cycles anyway | — |
| 10 | **Frame/time offsets** (frame 1 vs 0, fps_base, sub-frames, handlers) | wind state and camera pose belong to different instants | Blender's frame system is not used: every render is at an explicit time t = i·stride/fps that feeds both the wind model and the camera path | flow-vs-leaf-transform check (< 0.03 px) |
| 11 | **Blender's Vector (motion) pass with script-driven deformation** | motion vectors are silently wrong (Blender does not see our per-frame vertex writes) | Vector pass disabled; flow is computed from a per-vertex `P_next` attribute (exact next-frame position of each surface point) | `flow_and_leaf_tracks` check |
| 12 | **Procedural textures swim on deforming meshes** | bark texture slides over the wood under wind → photometric inconsistency | wood texture coordinates come from the rest-position attribute `P_rest` | visual |
| 13 | **Native motion blur / rolling shutter need depsgraph-level animation** | Blender would blur nothing (geometry is pushed per frame) | motion blur = average of sub-frame renders in linear light; rolling shutter = row bands rendered at their own times; GT stays at frame-centre time (like a global-shutter depth sensor) | metadata records both; RGB-alignment check is skipped when they are on |
| 14 | **Calm ≠ exact rest pose through round-off** | "calm" leaves move by 1e-16 and identity tests fail | displacement form v = v0 + Δ, element-wise Rodrigues gives exact I at angle 0 | `test_wind_calm_is_exact_rest`; pairing check: calm leaf transforms == identity bit-exactly |
| 15 | **Determinism across machines** | different Blender/numpy versions → different geometry | tree generated once (npz), wind/camera in float64 NumPy; verified bit-identical GT across Blender 4.5 (numpy 1.26) and 5.2 (numpy 2.3). Rendered *pixels* are deterministic per machine (fixed seeds) but not across GPUs | cross-version comparison (0 differing depth/id pixels) |
| 16 | **Nerfstudio re-orients and rescales the scene** | reconstructions are not in metres → geometry metrics meaningless | always train with `--orientation-method none --center-method none --auto-scale-poses False`; `eval_geometry.py --dataparser-transforms` can undo it otherwise | — |
| 17 | **TUM depth scale** | MonoGS/TUM default depth_scale 5000; ours is 1000 (mm) | converter writes `depth_scale: 1000.0` | — |
| 18 | **cv2 colour order** | RGB/BGR swaps in PNG I/O | all PNG I/O goes through `synthtree.imageio` (and the Kaggle reader), which converts to RGB | — |
| 19 | **Ground plane / room size vs orbit** | camera outside the room sees only a wall; ground edge visible | room half-size = orbit radius + 1.5 m with its own ceiling light; ground is 30 × 30 m | visual / validation |
| 20 | **macOS numpy 2.0 matmul warnings** | spurious "divide by zero in matmul" RuntimeWarnings on Apple Accelerate | hot paths use `einsum`; results verified finite | — |
